# Two Lines to Your Past Self

A warning to my past self about one specific moment — the 2 a.m. email
of 14 March 2019 — and why that moment mattered.

> On 14 March 2019, don't hit send on the 2 a.m. email to your boss — you meant 'let's talk Monday' and your thumb typed 'I quit'.
> Leaving that draft unsent would have saved eleven months of job hunting and the flat you had to give up.

Run with `node main.ts` (Node 22). The entry is 233 characters, inside
the 1–280 character gate.
